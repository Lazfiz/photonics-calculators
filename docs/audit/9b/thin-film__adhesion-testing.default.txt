Model

Benjamin–Weaver: a spherical stylus sinks into a fully plastic substrate until the mean contact pressure equals the hardness H, and τ is the shear stress at the leading edge of the contact, with the prefactor k = 1 (later analyses give 0.2–1, Bull & Berasetegui 2006, so τ is an upper estimate). It ranks coatings tested with one stylus; the critical load also depends on friction, film thickness, the stylus wear and the failure mode, so τ is not a work of adhesion. Use the substrate hardness from indentation at a similar depth (optical glasses ≈ 5–7 GPa).
