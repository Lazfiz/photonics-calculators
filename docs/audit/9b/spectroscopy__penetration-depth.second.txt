Normal incidence: α = 4πk/λ₀, intensity I(z) = I₀ e^(−αz), d_p = 1/α.

Oblique incidence from index n₀: k_z = (2π/λ₀) √(N² − n₀² sin²θ), N = n + ik, so d_p = λ₀ / (4π · Im √(N² − n₀² sin²θ)).

This one formula gives 1/α at normal incidence and the evanescent TIR depth for k = 0 above the critical angle. The intensity is the depth-dependent factor of the transmitted field; reflection losses at the surface are not included.
