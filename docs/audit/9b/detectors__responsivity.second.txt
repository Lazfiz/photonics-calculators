At fixed quantum efficiency the responsivity rises linearly with wavelength, because each watt carries more photons. Without internal gain η ≤ 1, so a datasheet responsivity above the η = 1 line is impossible for a PIN photodiode. Real detectors have η(λ) that falls at short and long wavelengths; see the spectral-response page.
