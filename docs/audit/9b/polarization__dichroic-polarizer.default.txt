Dichroic Polarizer

Model absorbing dichroic polarizers from the complex index of each principal axis: principal transmittances, extinction ratio and crossed-pair leakage.

Model: Exact

each principal axis: N = n − iκ,  α = 4πκ/λ,  R = ((n − 1)² + κ²)/((n + 1)² + κ²)

k = (1 − R)² e−αd/(1 − R² e−2αd)  (normal incidence, incoherent reflections)

extinction k₁/k₂,  efficiency (k₁ − k₂)/(k₁ + k₂),  crossed pair H₉₀ = k₁k₂

κ is the imaginary part of the index (absorption index), not an absorption coefficient: κ = 3 × 10⁻³ at 550 nm is α = 68.5 mm⁻¹. A metal has κ of order 1 to 10 and blocks both axes. The defaults are illustrative, not a measured material.

MODEL AND REFERENCES

Exact. The equations are exact within the stated assumptions, or are a standard's tabulated values. The results are as good as the inputs. About the tiers

Slab at normal incidence, N = n − iκ per principal axis, face reflectance including κ, multiple reflections added in power; ratios from logs. Not modelled: dispersion, scattering, oblique light.
