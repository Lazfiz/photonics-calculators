Model

θ = V B L

Sign convention (Vojna et al.): V < 0 when the medium rotates the polarization clockwise for light propagating along B. Tb³⁺ and Ce³⁺ paramagnets are negative, diamagnetic glass is positive. The rotation does not reverse with the beam, so a round trip doubles it.

|V| = F λ₀²/(λ² − λ₀²) + G

Single-transition fit of Vojna et al. 2019: TGG F 820.3 rad/(T·m), G −6.2, λ₀ 239.3 nm; TSAG F 756.7, λ₀ 262 nm; CeF₃ F 743.6, λ₀ 239 nm, stopped at 700 nm because it underestimates 1064 nm by 15 %. Fused silica uses V = α/(λ² − λ₀²) + β (Tran et al. 2024), fitted to 310 ± 3 °/(T·m) at 532 nm.

YIG is ferrimagnetic: its rotation saturates (304 rad/m at 1550 nm) and stops growing with B, so the field input has no effect on it.

MODEL AND REFERENCES

Textbook approximation. A standard approximation from the literature (for example paraxial, plane-wave or Gaussian-beam), valid within the range the page states. About the tiers

θ = VBL is exact for a uniform field. V(λ) at room temperature comes from published fits inside their ranges: the single-transition model for the Tb³⁺ and Ce³⁺ paramagnets (Vojna et al.), a dispersion fit for fused silica (Tran et al.). YIG is ferrimagnetic, so only its saturated rotation at 1550 nm is given.
