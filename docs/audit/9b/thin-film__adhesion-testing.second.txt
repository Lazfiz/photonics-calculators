Model

A film on the convex side of a wide substrate bent to radius R carries ε = hs/(2R) with no strain across the bend (plane strain). G = Z σ²h/Ēffrom Hutchinson & Suo for a film much thinner than the substrate: Z = 1.976 for a channel crack with matched film and substrate moduli, ½ for steady-state delamination. A substrate more compliant than the film (a polymer) raises the channel-crack Z, and a stiffer one lowers it; the delamination G doesn't depend on the substrate. Residual stress from deposition and thermal mismatch, often hundreds of MPa in optical coatings, adds to the bending stress. A film in net compression (or on the concave side) buckles instead, which isn't modelled.
