Key Formulas

Jones matrix: R(−θ) · diag(1, e−iΓ) · R(θ), R(θ) = [[cos θ, sin θ], [−sin θ, cos θ]]

Γ = retardance, θ = fast axis angle

Stokes: S₀ = |Eₓ|² + |E_y|², S₁ = |Eₓ|² − |E_y|²

S₂ = 2 Re(Eₓ* E_y), S₃ = 2 Im(Eₓ* E_y)

MODEL AND REFERENCES

Exact. The equations are exact within the stated assumptions, or are a standard's tabulated values. The results are as good as the inputs. About the tiers

Ideal lossless linear retarder R(−θ)·diag(1, e^(−iΓ))·R(θ) on linearly polarized light, Goldstein convention (S₃ > 0 is right-handed). Not modelled: dispersion of Γ, reflections, tilt.
