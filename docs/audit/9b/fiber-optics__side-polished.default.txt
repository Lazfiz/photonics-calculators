ψ(r) = J₀(U) K₀(Wr/a)/K₀(W) in the cladding; η = ∫_(x > a+d) ψ² dA / ∫ψ² dA

κ(D) = (NA/(n₁a)) (U²/V³) K₀(WD/a)/K₁²(W), D(z) = 2(a + d) + z²/R, P₂ = sin²(∫κ dz)

The exact LP01 mode of the unpolished step-index fibre (eigenvalues U, W) gives the field beyond the polished face, which lies a distance d from the core boundary. Its tail decays as K₀(Wr/a), faster near the core than a pure exponential. η is the fraction of the mode power on the far side of the face; an overlay of index n_ov shifts n_eff to first order by η(n_ov² − n₂²)/(2n_eff).

Coupler: two identical fibres, each bent to radius R in a block and polished, are placed face to face. Their core spacing grows as z²/R away from the centre, so the coupling κ (Snyder's coefficient) acts over an effective length close to Digonnet and Shaw's √(πaR/W). The fibres are identical, so the transfer is complete when ∫κ dz = π/2.

Not included: the change of the mode itself near the face (first order overestimates the shift for n_ov < n₂ and underestimates it as n_ov approaches n_eff), polarization (scalar LP model; polished-fibre polarizers rely on it), polishing scatter and roughness, and a gap index different from n₂ in the coupler.
