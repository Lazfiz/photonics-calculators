θ = atan(N t/L). Four-level gain: P_th = δ h c A/(2η_p σ τ λ_p), A = W t cos θ, δ = −ln R_oc + 2α_s L/cos θ; output from Rigrod with the mode filling the slab.

ΔT(y) = Q(t²/4 − y²)/(2κ). A straight-through ray sees n(y) = n₀ − (dn/dT)Qy²/(2κ), a lens f = κ/((dn/dT)QL); each zigzag ray samples the whole profile, so this lens and its depolarisation cancel to first order (stress and end effects are not modelled).

MODEL AND REFERENCES

Textbook approximation. A standard approximation from the literature (for example paraxial, plane-wave or Gaussian-beam), valid within the range the page states. About the tiers

Four-level gain with uniform inversion over the slab, the zigzag beam filling the aperture W t cos θ, Rigrod output. Infinite-slab heat conduction with uniform heat density; the straight-through lens keeps only dn/dT (no stress-optic, end-bulging or edge effects). Heat fraction 0.32: Fan's (1993) estimate for Nd:YAG pumped at 808 nm and lasing at 1064 nm (non-lasing, he measured 0.37–0.43).
