The pulse train repeats once per cavity round trip, f_rep = c/2L (linear) or c/L (ring), with L the optical length. A transform-limited pulse of intensity FWHM τ spans Δν = K/τ, K = 0.4413 (Gaussian) or 0.3148 (sech²), so about Δν/f_rep modes oscillate in phase. A chirped pulse is longer than this limit for the same bandwidth. Peak power: 0.939 E/τ (Gaussian), 0.881 E/τ (sech²).

MODEL AND REFERENCES

Textbook approximation. A standard approximation from the literature (for example paraxial, plane-wave or Gaussian-beam), valid within the range the page states. About the tiers

Transform-limited pulses: Δν·τ = 0.4413 (Gaussian) or 0.3148 (sech²), intensity FWHMs. The mode count is the number of modes f_rep apart inside Δν. Peak power from the shape factor, energy P/f_rep. Chirp, pedestals and the mode-locking mechanism itself are not modelled.
