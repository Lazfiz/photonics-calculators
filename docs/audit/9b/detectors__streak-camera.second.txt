Key Formulas

Δtslit = wslit·M / vsweep; Δtpixel = p / vsweep

Gaussian terms: ΔtG = √(Δttube² + Δtjitter² + (b/v)²) (Gaussians add in quadrature)

Δt = FWHM of rect(Δtslit) ⊗ rect(Δtpixel) ⊗ Gauss(ΔtG); no Gaussian: max(Δtslit, Δtpixel)

Twindow = min(Npixels·p, Lscreen) / vsweep; a FWHM below 2p/v is undersampled

Blur: Gaussian MTF exp(−2π²σ²f²) = 5 % at the limiting resolution f, FWHM = 2.3548·√(ln 20 / 2)/(π f) at the cathode (26.2 µm at 35 lp/mm), times M on the screen

Tube spread: the tube's own Gaussian term, chiefly transit-time dispersion from the photoelectron energy spread, ≈ 2.63·√Δε / E ps (Δε FWHM of the photoelectron energy distribution in eV, extraction field E in kV/mm; Shakya & Chang 2005 after Chang et al. 1996), plus deflection dispersion. The delay between electrons leaving the cathode with 0 and with Δε, √(2m_eΔε)/(eE) = 3.37·√Δε / E ps, bounds it from above. A data-sheet resolution includes the slit and readout, so it bounds the tube term from above.

MODEL AND REFERENCES

Textbook approximation. A standard approximation from the literature (for example paraxial, plane-wave or Gaussian-beam), valid within the range the page states. About the tiers

Slit image and pixel are rectangles, tube, blur and jitter Gaussians; exact FWHM of their convolution. Blur: 5 % Gaussian MTF at the cathode limit, × M. Trigger jitter is entered as FWHM. Defaults: C16910, 60 ps sweep.
