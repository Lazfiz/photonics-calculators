MODEL AND REFERENCES

Textbook approximation. A standard approximation from the literature (for example paraxial, plane-wave or Gaussian-beam), valid within the range the page states. About the tiers

Each trap level empties exponentially, so the afterpulse probability is Σ η·e^(−t_d/τ) over up to two levels, with the recorded rate from a non-paralyzable dead time. η and τ are device fits (the defaults give 0.41 %, the order of a Si SPCM at 50 ns), and the afterpulse rate is first order in R·τ: afterpulses of afterpulses are left out.

Cova S., Lacaita A., Ripamonti G. (1991). Trapping phenomena in avalanche photodiodes on nanosecond scale. IEEE Electron Device Lett. 12, 685–687. Afterpulsing from trap release, η·e^(−t/τ). doi:10.1109/55.116955
