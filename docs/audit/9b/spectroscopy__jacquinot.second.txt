The highest wavenumber in the band sets the field stop: a ray at angle θ sees the path difference x cos θ, so its lines appear at ν̃ cos θ; the field is limited to the cone where that shift stays below the resolution.

MODEL AND REFERENCES

Textbook approximation. A standard approximation from the literature (for example paraxial, plane-wave or Gaussian-beam), valid within the range the page states. About the tiers

Geometric optics: unapodized resolution 1/L, circular field stop, grating spectrometer in Littrow limited by its slit width; both instruments share the beam area.
