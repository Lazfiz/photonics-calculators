Each cavity is (HL)p S (LH)p with a half-wave spacer S; cavities are coupled by a quarter-wave L layer. Exact transfer matrix at normal incidence from air, lossless and non-dispersive layers, back surface ignored. FWHM: from λ₀ outward to where T first falls to half the peak, found by bisection. There are no matching layers, so the peak T is below 100 % and a multi-cavity pass band ripples (Macleod, Thin-Film Optical Filters, ch. 8).
